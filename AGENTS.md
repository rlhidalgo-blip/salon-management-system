## 1. Project Overview

Yab's Salon Management System is an internal web application for Yab's Hair and Beauty Studio.

The system aims to replace manual sales recording, commission calculations, and financial reporting with a centralized, reliable application.

This is a real business product being developed through AI-assisted software engineering.
